The people behind Fryda

The story behind the counter

Fryda was born from the wish for a warm little corner in downtown Taubaté — where coffee is brewed slowly, food is made with care and everyone is welcomed like family. Behind it, two women.

Owner

Maria

The most emblematic figure of the house ❤️

Maria is the definition of strength, optimism, high spirits and good conversation. She believes in Fryda more than anyone — she traded her well-earned retirement to be here, welcoming everyone with love, every single day.

With a smile and a simple "Hi, have you been here before? Make yourself at home," she turned countless guests into Frydalovers (and Marialovers). As she likes to say, there are many "Amoras" out there — and she cares for each one as if they were her own blood.

Maria teaches us, every day, that you need strength, grit and drive — always. All our admiration and honor for working with you. ❤️

Owner & Head chef

Renata

The hand behind our kitchen

Owner of Fryda and the chef of the house, Renata signs everything that comes out of the kitchen: the risottos in natural broth, the melt-in-your-mouth gnocchi and the daily lunch — all made fresh, with technique and the same warmth that drives Fryda.

She also looks after every detail of the menu, so that each dish tastes like real, homemade food — the kind that makes you want to come back.
